## Facts

- Snow is mostly trapped air and insulates well; an igloo warmed by body heat alone can sit between about -7 C and +16 C inside while it is far colder outside (Wikipedia 'Igloo'; verify wording before voice). 'Almost forty degrees warmer' is within that range against -40 outside.
- Igloos have a small vent hole in the roof, and a sunken entrance (a cold trap) because cold air sinks and stays below the sleeping level (Wikipedia 'Igloo'; verify before voice).
- Forty below is the same in Celsius and Fahrenheit.
- Tent poles snapping in a long blizzard and a battery heater dying: scenario logic, exaggerated for comedy.
