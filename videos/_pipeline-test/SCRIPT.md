## Facts

- Toast falling off a normal-height table only has time for about half a turn, so it usually lands butter-side down (R. A. J. Matthews, 'Tumbling toast, Murphy's Law and the fundamental constants', Eur. J. Phys. 16 (1995) 172, doi:10.1088/0143-0807/16/4/005).
- A table about 10 feet (3 m) tall would give it time for a full turn and a butter-side-up landing (Matthews 1995, as summarised by Wikipedia 'Buttered toast phenomenon').
